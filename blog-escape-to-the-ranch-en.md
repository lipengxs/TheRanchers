## More Than Just a Farming Game

What sets The Ranchers apart isn't just polished farming mechanics—it's the sheer scope. You'll clear land, plant crops, and tend to animals, but also explore wild islands, delve into mines, uncover shipwrecks, and brave dungeons where monsters guard rare resources. Travel on foot, horseback, scooter, pickup truck, or yacht. The game balances peaceful farm life with the thrill of discovery: one moment you're managing crops, the next you're battling creatures for crafting materials.

## Animal Husbandry & Building

Players can raise mammal animals and oviparous animals, each requiring specific housing—barns for livestock, coops for poultry. Auto-feeding kits, water management, and breeding mechanics let you expand your herds. The building system allows creative ranch layouts: water towers provide coverage for crops and animals, workshops enable crafting and tool upgrades. Learn more about [animals and buildings](https://the-ranchers.com/wiki) in our wiki.

## Modern Features: Auction Market & Multiplayer

The Ranchers features an online auction market accessible in-game and via a companion mobile app on iOS and Android—check prices and manage trades anywhere. Players can also build wind turbines and solar panels, sign utility contracts, and sell excess electricity to the village. Perhaps the most compelling feature is seamless 1–4 player co-op: friends can join your ranch anytime to develop your property, complete goals, or explore together. Ranch management becomes more enjoyable when you're building something with others.
