Short answer

The live Steam page should be checked for the current store state. How to Check Unlock Status

Open the Steam page in the same account and region you plan to use. If the page still says the game is not available, do not assume the launch is cancelled; Steam unlocks can roll over by store timing, local cache, or account region.

The most useful note to publish is the state you can verify: local date, region, button text, and whether the store has changed from wishlist to install or purchase. Avoid inventing an exact hour unless Steam or the developer publishes one.

  • Refresh the Steam app, not only the browser page.
  • Check whether your region shows a price or install option.
  • Confirm that you are viewing The Ranchers app page, not an old demo or community post.

First-Hour Troubleshooting

If Steam still shows Coming Soon after other players report access, restart the Steam client, clear the download cache only after simpler checks, and compare with the official community hub before reinstalling anything. For co-op, each player should confirm the same build and online status before troubleshooting invites.

Launch-Day Checklist

  1. Open the live Steam page and note the button text.
  2. Check whether price, install, or purchase state is visible in your region.
  3. Restart Steam if the browser and client disagree.
  4. Confirm the game version before starting co-op.
  5. After first launch, record save behavior, tutorial flow, and which systems are available.

FAQ

Is The Ranchers out today?

Check the live official store for the current release or purchase state. Check the live Steam page in your region for the actual unlock state.

Why does Steam still show Coming Soon?

The store may not have unlocked in your region yet, or the Steam client may need a refresh. Check the app page and community hub before changing local files.
