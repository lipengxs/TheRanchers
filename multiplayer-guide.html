Safe session checklist

  1. Confirm that every player is using the same current game version.
  2. Use only an invite or session method explicitly supported by the current official source or in-game UI.
  3. Before changing a save, make a backup and confirm who owns or hosts it.
  4. Check official notices for maintenance or known service issues.
  5. Do not delete a save or modify local files as a first response to an invite problem.
