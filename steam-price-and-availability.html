Before you purchase, wishlist or download

  1. Read the action button: purchase, install, wishlist or another current store state.
  2. Confirm the displayed price and currency in your own Steam region.
  3. Read the edition and included-content wording, if any.
  4. Check the store date, language, compatibility and refund terms before making a decision.
  5. For a changed listing, look for an official news post before relying on search snippets or old screenshots.
