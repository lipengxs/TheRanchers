The Ranchers System Requirements

These requirements reflect the current Steam AppDetails listing checked on June 30, 2026. The Ranchers is listed for Early Access on July 30, 2026 with Windows, Mac, and Linux support.

Minimum PC Specs

  • OS: Windows 7+ / 8.1 / 10 64-bit
  • CPU: Intel i3 Processor
  • Memory: 8 GB RAM
  • GPU: Nvidia GTX 660 / AMD Radeon R9 270
  • Storage: 10 GB available space

Recommended PC Specs

  • OS: Windows 10 64-bit
  • CPU: Intel i7 Processor
  • Memory: 16 GB RAM
  • GPU: Nvidia GTX 960+ / AMD Radeon R9 380+
  • Storage: 12 GB available space

Mac & Linux Minimum

  • Mac OS: Mac OSX 10.10+
  • Mac CPU: 4-core CPU, Apple M1 or higher
  • Mac memory: 8 GB RAM
  • Linux OS: Ubuntu 12.04 LTS+
  • Linux memory: 8 GB RAM
  • Storage: 10 GB available space

Setup Notes

The Ranchers is an open-world country-life sim with vehicles, ranch layouts, and co-op planning, so performance may vary by farm density and build complexity. For gameplay prep, continue with demo route and ranch-building guides.
