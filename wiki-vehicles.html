Wiki / Vehicles

🚗 Vehicles

Travel across the 16 km² open world

Overview

The Ranchers features diverse transportation options. With a vast 16 km² map including mines, islands, and dungeons, vehicles are essential for efficient exploration and resource hauling.

🐴 Horse

Classic ranch transport. Fast and maneuverable on land.

🛵 Scooter

Compact and agile. Great for quick trips around Sunvale.

🛻 Pickup Truck

Essential for hauling. Large cargo bed for crops, resources, and tools. Prioritize unlocking this early.

🛥️ Yacht

Explore seas and islands. Reach shipwrecks and remote locations.

🚶 On Foot

Always available. Use when vehicles aren't practical.

Tips

  • Save up for the pickup truck early—its cargo capacity greatly improves efficiency.
  • Use the scooter for quick errands in town.
  • The yacht unlocks water exploration and hidden island content.
  • Repair or purchase vehicles at Wanglow's Garage.

Vehicle Route Planning

Vehicles are not just speed upgrades in The Ranchers. They decide how much work fits into a day. A good route starts at storage, passes the task location, and ends with a clean return path so crops, ore, animal products, or shop purchases do not sit in the wrong place overnight.

Farm to Sunvale Loop

Use the scooter or pickup for shop visits, villager errands, and quick supply runs. Park facing the exit so the return trip starts fast.

Mine Hauling Loop

Bring the pickup when resource trips matter. Keep tools and empty storage space ready before leaving the farm.

Animal Supply Loop

Use vehicle storage for feed, building materials, and ranch upgrades so animal districts can be improved in fewer trips.

My Garage and Parking Notes

Recent demo updates made vehicle access and garage management more important for everyday play. Treat My Garage as a planning hub: keep the vehicle you use most closest to your storage route, and avoid parking in places that block crop, barn, or machine movement.

  • Early game: prioritize one reliable town vehicle and one cargo option.
  • Mid game: park near storage, road exits, and frequently used machines.
  • Co-op: assign one player to vehicle logistics so tools and materials do not scatter across the map.
  • Subway travel: use subway access for repeated long routes, then keep vehicles for the last-mile farm or resource run.
