The Ranchers World Map Guide

The Ranchers is an open-world country-life sim, so the map is a time-management problem as much as a place. The best layout gets you from farm chores to town errands, mines, vehicles, animals, and co-op jobs without wasting half the day.

Core Map Zones

  • Home farm: crop blocks, animal districts, storage, and machines.
  • Sunvale City: shops, villagers, errands, and relationship routines.
  • Mines: resource trips, danger prep, and tool planning.
  • Road network: vehicle routes, parking, garage access, and fast return paths.
  • Remote areas and islands: yacht routes and late-route exploration.

Vehicle Route Planning

Driving updates, My Garage, parking, and subway travel turn the map into a route puzzle. Put vehicles near storage and exits, then use subway access for repeated town loops.

Farm Layout Map

Build districts rather than scattered decorations: animal care near feeders, crop blocks near water, machines near storage, and garage access near the road.

Co-op Map Roles

In 1-4 player sessions, split the world by routes: one player handles animals, one handles crops, one mines, and one does town or vehicle logistics. Continue with the beginner guide.
